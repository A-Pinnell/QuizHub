Keyboard guide

Main menu
Arrow keys move between courses; Page Up/Down changes course pages. Enter or Space opens a focused control.
Menus and options
O opens or closes Options; P opens Party Mode. Tab and Shift+Tab move between controls in a menu. Arrow keys change radio, select, and slider choices. Escape closes a menu or returns from quiz setup.
Quiz
Arrow keys or 1–4 / A–D focus answers. Enter selects an answer, then advances after it is answered. Escape ends the quiz.
Sound
M toggles sound effects and music.
Global quiz theme
Sound effects style
Default uses PS2 effects in Computer Graphics and QuizHub effects elsewhere. PS2 Style uses available PS2 effects in every quiz. This setting is separate from quiz themes and the mute toggle.
Motion
FullMinimalReduced
Minimal keeps animations quick with fewer visible frames. Reduced turns most motion off.
