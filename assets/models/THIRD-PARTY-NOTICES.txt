The short-answer semantic checker includes:

- all-MiniLM-L6-v2 ONNX model, from Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  The model is licensed under Apache License 2.0.
- Transformers.js, from Hugging Face
  https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js
  Licensed under Apache License 2.0.
- ONNX Runtime Web
  https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
  Licensed under MIT License.

The model and runtime execute locally in the browser. Short-answer text is not
sent to the model publisher or any other remote service.
